# HASI v0.828d

HASI human associated consensus and sovereign cooperation

This release presents the full v0.828d white paper and technical summary for public and technical review.

HASI proposes human-associated consensus weight among increasingly capable AI systems, together with sovereign personal control, guarded information release and voluntary cooperation. Copying an AI agent must not create another human allocation. The proposed default reserves the human base before age 18 and requires adult eligibility and authorization before exercise.

- Technical summary: 9-page PDF, editable Word document and Markdown reading copy.

## Review focus

We invite counterexamples and reproducible analysis of personhood evidence, replica accounting, consensus assumptions, guardian enforcement, privacy, incentives and defensive coscaling. The paper states conditional arguments and unresolved research obligations; this release does not announce a completed implementation or general ASI safety guarantee.
