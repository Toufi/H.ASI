# Contributing to HASI

HASI is open for public and technical review. Corrections, counterexamples and reproduced failures are useful contributions.

## Submit a review

Open a GitHub issue with the [technical review template](review/REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md). Identify the document version, section or equation, the claim being examined, your assumptions, and the reasoning or evidence supporting your finding. State what correction or test would resolve it.

For proposed wording changes, submit a pull request against the relevant Markdown file. Explain any change to the mathematical or technical meaning, and identify corresponding passages in the Word and PDF documents that would need updating in a subsequent manuscript version.

## Priority questions

- Can human uniqueness, liveness, maturity and voluntary authorization be established under an explicit threat model while preserving privacy?
- Can replicas, delegation, recovery or cross-domain enrollment multiply a human's counted allocation?
- What fault and synchrony assumptions are necessary for consensus and finality?
- Can guardian coalitions, substituted outputs or stale evidence defeat the human's disclosure and spending limits?
- What incentives remain under human coercion, credential rental, concentration and strong outside options?
- Which experiments distinguish helpful combinations with existing safety methods from correlated failures?

## Review standards

Critique claims and constructions rather than attributing motives to participants. Separate a mathematical argument from an empirical result or a proposed experiment. Include runnable examples and exact versions where relevant. Do not publish private human records or credentials in an issue or pull request.

Please contribute only text and materials you have permission to share under this repository's documentation license, CC BY-SA 4.0. Software implementations may require their own expressly stated licenses; the white paper's license does not establish the license of a referenced component.

The maintainers will decide which changes to incorporate. A submission does not establish validation, endorsement, funding or implementation status.
